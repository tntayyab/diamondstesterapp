## Full description
Not sure what spider you just found? Point your camera, snap a photo, and Spider
Identifier uses AI to tell you the likely species, its family, and — most
importantly — how dangerous it is.

FEATURES
• AI photo identification — species, family and a clear venom-risk indicator
• Ask the Spider Expert — an AI chatbot for any spider question
• Species library — 18+ common spiders with photos, habitat and bite risk, works offline
• Field guides & blog — research-checked identification guides
• Bite first-aid, web-type and anatomy references
• Identification quiz to sharpen your eye
• Community feed — share your finds, follow other spider fans
• Field journal — log sightings with location

Spider Identifier is for education and curiosity. AI results are informational and
are not a substitute for professional medical or pest-control advice. If you are
bitten and feel unwell, seek medical help immediately.
