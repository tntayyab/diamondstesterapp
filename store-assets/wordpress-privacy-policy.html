Last updated: 17 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Spider Identifier (published by AppsRocks24) collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to both our website (spideridentifier.online) and our Spider Identifier mobile app for Android and iOS. We keep data collection to the minimum needed to run the service. Contact: hellospideridentifier@gmail.com.

Information we collect

  • Account information. If you create an account, we store your email address and an encrypted password, handled by our backend provider Supabase. Your display name and an optional profile photo are stored only if you choose to add them.
  • Photos you submit. When you identify a spider, the image you upload or capture is sent through our secure server to our AI provider, Anthropic, to analyse it and return a result. Photos are used to generate your identification and are never used to advertise to you. Photos you deliberately share to the community feed are stored and shown publicly.
  • Location (mobile app, optional). If you grant permission in the app, approximate or precise location is used to tag field-journal sightings and to show which species occur near you. You can decline this and still use the app.
  • Community content. Posts, comments, likes and favourites you create are stored and may be visible to other users.
  • Usage analytics. Basic, largely anonymous analytics such as pages or features used, to understand aggregate usage and improve the product.

How we use information

  • To process your photo and return a species match with a confidence score and venom-risk indicator.
  • To create and secure your account, keep your history, and run the community feed.
  • To respond to your messages and, if you opt in, to send occasional product and guide updates.
  • To show rewarded ads that grant in-app credits (mobile app only), monitor performance, prevent abuse, and improve accuracy over time. We do not sell your personal data.

Image handling

Uploaded images are used to generate your result. Where storage is enabled, images are optimised and retained only as long as needed to operate the feature — or, if you post to the community, until you delete the post. You can request deletion of any image or account data you have provided by contacting us.

Advertising (mobile app)

The Android and iOS app shows Google AdMob rewarded ads. AdMob may collect your device advertising ID and related device information to serve and measure ads. We request non-personalised ads by default. The website does not show third-party ads.

Cookies & analytics (website)

On the website we use essential cookies to make the site function and may use privacy-respecting analytics to understand aggregate usage. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Payments

  • Website subscriptions are processed by Paddle.com, our authorised reseller and Merchant of Record, under its own privacy policy.
  • In-app subscriptions are processed by the app store you purchase through — Google Play Billing or the Apple App Store — with RevenueCat helping us manage entitlements.
  • In all cases we never see or store your full card details.

Third-party services

We rely on reputable providers to operate the service: Supabase (accounts, database and storage), Anthropic (AI processing of your submitted photo or question), Google AdMob (advertising in the mobile app), and Paddle, Google Play Billing / RevenueCat and Apple (payments). These providers process data on our behalf under their own security commitments, and data is transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

Data retention & deletion

You can edit or remove your profile and individual posts at any time from your dashboard on the website or the settings screen in the app.

To delete your account and all associated data, follow the steps on our Delete Account page or email hellospideridentifier@gmail.com, and we will action the request within 30 days.

Children

Spider Identifier is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information, and to object to certain processing. Contact us to exercise these rights.

Contact

For any privacy question or request, email hellospideridentifier@gmail.com.
