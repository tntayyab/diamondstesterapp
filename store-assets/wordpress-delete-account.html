Last updated: 17 July 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your Spider Identifier account (app published by AppsRocks24) and all data associated with it. It applies to the Spider Identifier mobile app for Android and iOS and to the Spider Identifier website at spideridentifier.online.

How to request deletion

To delete your Spider Identifier account and its associated data, send an email to hellospideridentifier@gmail.com from the email address registered to your account, with the subject line "Delete my account".

So we can find and verify your account, please include the email address (or display name) you use in the app. You do not need to give a reason.

We verify the request and permanently delete your account within 30 days, and email you a confirmation once it is done. There is no charge for this.

What data is deleted

When we action your request we permanently delete:

  • Your account and login credentials (email and encrypted password)
  • Your display name and profile photo
  • Your identification history and saved species or favourites
  • Your community posts, comments and likes
  • Any field-journal entries and location tags
  • The photos you have uploaded

What data may be kept, and for how long

  • We may retain a limited amount of data where the law requires it — for example records of purchases and payments needed for tax, accounting and fraud-prevention. This is kept only for the period required by law and is not used for any other purpose.
  • Anonymised or aggregated data that can no longer identify you, such as overall usage statistics, may be kept to help us improve the service.
  • Residual copies may remain in our encrypted backups for a short period and are automatically purged, typically within 30 days.

Deleting individual content without closing your account

If you do not want to delete your whole account, you can remove individual community posts, comments, or profile details at any time from within the app or on the website.

Questions

If you have any question about account or data deletion, email hellospideridentifier@gmail.com and we will help.
